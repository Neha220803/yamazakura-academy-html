What a corporate engagement looks like

Training is designed around the team's actual work — the meetings they sit in, the documents they read, the colleagues they speak to. Sessions run on site or online, at a pace the schedule can absorb.

一Requirement study with the L&D or engineering lead before anything is scheduled
二A curriculum matched to the team's starting level and target JLPT band
三Live sessions with conversation practice and workplace etiquette in every class
四Periodic assessment and a progress report you can take to your stakeholders
