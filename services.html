JLPT Preparation

N5

Beginner

Hiragana, katakana, basic kanji and everyday phrases. Your first real conversations.

N4

Elementary

Wider vocabulary and grammar for daily situations, plus comfortable reading.

N3

Intermediate

The bridge level — natural speech, longer texts, workplace Japanese.

Advanced mastery for business communication, interviews and professional work.

FAQs

Before you enquire

Do I need any prior Japanese to join?

No. The N5 batch starts from hiragana and katakana, assuming nothing at all. If you have studied before, a short placement conversation decides which level suits you.

Are the classes online or in person?

Training runs online, and corporate programs can also be delivered on site at your office. Both formats use the same curriculum and the same conversation practice.

How long does each JLPT level take?

It depends on the batch pace and how much practice happens between sessions. Duration for the batch you are considering is confirmed when you enquire.

Is there a free demo class?

Yes — a free demo class is available so you can see the teaching before committing to a batch.

What are the fees?

Fees vary by level and batch format. Share which level you are aiming for and we will send the current fee structure.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us the situation and we'll point you to the right one. We reply within one working day.
