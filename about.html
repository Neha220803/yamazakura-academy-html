Our Approach

Four stages, repeated every level

The same cycle runs from N5 through N2. Nothing is learned that isn't then spoken, used, and built on.

01学

Learn

Grammar, vocabulary and script introduced in the order they're actually needed to hold a conversation.

02習

Practice

Spoken drills every session. Reading and listening built around the same material so nothing sits idle.

03用

Apply

Workplace scenarios, etiquette, and the cultural context that decides whether a sentence lands well.

04伸

Grow

Assessment, JLPT readiness, and the next level — or the interview the language was learned for.
